"TAP" can't be a distinctive brand, and a standalone TAP logo invites trouble with the airline. Keep it where it is today:
- code namespaces, the exe and the save folders;
- text that has already used the full name once.

Don't file it.

## Recommendations

1. **Keep "The Astraea Program"** (D-23). The full name goes in the title, the logo and the store page. "TAP" is only an abbreviation.
2. **File an EU trade mark** for the word mark THE ASTRAEA PROGRAM, in classes 9 and 41.
   - When: in M1, and at the latest before the Steam "Coming soon" page (REL-04). The name is already public on GitHub, which is a reason not to wait.
   - Cost: the EUIPO online fee is €850 for one class plus €50 for the second.
   - EU-based applicants can file online without a representative.
3. **Extend to the US within 6 months**, the priority period that keeps the EU filing date.
   - Either through WIPO (Madrid, based on the EU filing) or directly at the USPTO ($350 per class).
   - An applicant living outside the US needs a US-licensed attorney to file directly.
   - Expect the examiner to raise ASTRAE ORATIO. The answer is the different full name.
4. **Talk to a trademark lawyer** briefly before filing, and bring this file. Ask about:
   - the class 41 "entertainment" wording of EUTM 017911873;
   - the US risk from ASTRAE ORATIO;
   - the wording of your own goods list.
5. **Funding.** The EUIPO SME Fund reimburses 75% of EU filing fees for EU-based SMEs, but the 2026 trade mark vouchers are used up. The fund has run every year since 2021. If there is a 2027 round, the voucher must be granted before you file. It's worth waiting for only if filing can wait until then.
6. **Repeat this search** right before filing, and again before the store page goes live.
7. **Register theastraeaprogram.com** now, and astraeaprogram.com if you like.
