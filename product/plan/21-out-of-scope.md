# 21 · Out of scope for 1.0, and later candidates

| Item | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spaceplanes and aircraft: hangar, runway, wings, jet engines, air intakes, landing gear | **Not now** (D-15) | TAP is a space game. The hub leaves room to add them later without moving anything. |
| Multiple planetary systems | **After 1.0: expansion X1** (D-17) | Prepared by FND-15; design questions in [20-multi-system-and-trade.md](20-multi-system-and-trade.md). |
| Trade routes | **After 1.0: expansion X2** (D-17) | Prepared by RES-01/04 and CAR-01; you will define them. |
| Comms network: relay satellites, signal range and delay | After 1.0 (D-22) | KSP added it after its 1.0. It pairs well with multi-system. |
| macOS build | After 1.0 (D-19) | Unity supports it; it needs a Mac for testing and notarisation. |
| Consoles | Not planned | Needs a full gamepad UI and certification. |
| Steam Deck "Verified" | After 1.0 (D-18) | "Playable" is the Early Access target (REL-07). |
| Multiplayer | Not planned | KSP never shipped it in the base game either. |
| Robotics (hinges, pistons, rotors) | Later candidate | A KSP expansion item. It would need joints inside a vessel, which conflicts with D-14. |
| Deployable surface experiments | Later candidate | A KSP expansion item; SCI could host it. |
| Comets | Later candidate | A variant of asteroids (RES-05). |
| Part variants (one part, several looks or sizes) | Later candidate | Useful once the catalogue is large. |
| EVA construction (attaching parts on EVA) | Later candidate | Big, and needs D-14 thinking. |
| Languages other than English | After 1.0 (D-12) | FND-10 makes it data-only. |
| Steam Workshop | After 1.0 | MOD-01 content packs are the base. |
| Mission builder (player-made scenarios) | Later candidate | UX-06's objectives system is the base. |
