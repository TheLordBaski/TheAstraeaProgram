# Planet visuals implementation

## Graphics overhaul (2026-10-02)

The user reported that the upgrade "didn't succeed as much" compared with Kitten Space Agency, and that they had tried
Ultra as well. A matched baseline (local before/after gallery in `Screenshots/PlanetOverhaul/`, not versioned) showed the causes:

| Finding | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black daytime sky at the pad, no blue limb from orbit (High/Ultra) | Sky radiance missed the factor pi between renderer light units and irradiance, and had almost no multiple scattering | Physically scaled sun irradiance; Hillaire multiple-scattering LUT; Bruneton transmittance LUT in half floats (was 8-bit) |
| The user's Low preset showed the old analytic shell | Scattering was disabled on Low | Every preset uses the scattering sky; Low integrates 10 samples (0.1–0.2 ms) |
| Leopard-spot clouds at 80 km; blanket-like volumes | 3D value noise, a hard threshold on it, and a shape remap spanning only 0.68–0.95 | Tiling Perlin-Worley 128³ + Worley 64³ noise; shape stretched to 0–1; edge-only redistribution in layers |
| Smudgy translucent clouds, cream undersides | Optical depth ~2; Beer-only light march left bases black | Layer optical depth 20 with two-stream lighting; volumes add diffuse transmission; white-balanced grade |
| Cartoon surface | Flat terrain-rule colours with a pixel noise field | 8K albedo: each Tellus colour rule painted with real landscape texture (NASA Blue Marble exemplars), Tellus geography unchanged |
| No detail between orbit and the ground | Orbital map ~0.5 km/px; ground materials only within 1.6 km | Three satellite-texture octaves (105, 26, 6.5 m/texel) fade in as each is resolved; ground materials to 2.5 km with their light/dark structure kept |
| Flat navy ocean, cartoon beach outline | Single colour, unnormalised glint | Depth-based shallow/deep colour, GGX sun glint widening with distance |
| Line along the dateline | Automatic mips on atan2 coordinates | Seamless gradients (Tarini) |
| 3.5 ms cloud cost at 80 km | Volumes ray-marched far above the deck | Volumes fade out between 12 and 42 km (0.02–0.07 radii); full-resolution layer clouds in the composite |

Atmosphere: Tellus uses Earth's scattering profile (Rayleigh 5.8/13.5/33.1e-6 per m, 8 km scale height; Mie 6e-6,
1.2 km, g 0.8) on its 600 km radius, which reads as a full Kerbin-like atmosphere. The integration splits at the 2D
cloud layer for exact aerial perspective in front of and behind it, and concentrates samples at the limb's tangent
point, the ground or the camera. Bodies without a current bake (custom worlds) get the tables generated at load, with
coefficients from their air column and authored sky colour. Physics atmosphere is unchanged.

Clouds: Tellus uses the NASA cloud composite as a whole-planet map (`WeatherMode = Global`: seed-rotated, polar ice
and haze thinned); Regional mode remains for other worlds. Layers: per-pixel two-stream reflectance/transmission,
cloud-top normals, silver lining and skylight, drawn at full resolution. Volumes: Schneider-style shape/erosion with
diffuse transmission, filtered to the pixel rather than the ray step.

Surface: `SatelliteAlbedo` profiles bake an 8192x4096 map (`SurfaceAlbedoSynth`). The mask's alpha stores the dominant
landscape class for the detail octaves. Source data and credits: [PLANET_VISUALS_SOURCES.md](PLANET_VISUALS_SOURCES.md).

Grade: ACES, post exposure +0.25, contrast +6, saturation -3, white balance -9/+3, bloom 0.6.

Editor diagnostics (RTX 3070 Laptop, Direct3D12, 1600x900 camera renders, busy PC, not a benchmark): total GPU frame
3.6–6.0 ms on Low, 3.6–7.7 ms on High, 3.6–8.4 ms on Ultra across pad, 1.5 km, 8 km, 80 km, 300 km and globe views;
the atmosphere/cloud integration 0.1–0.2 ms (Low) to 2.0 ms (Ultra, inside the cloud deck), composite 0.16–0.33 ms.
EditMode: 107/107 passed, including two new sky-table tests and the recorded terrain fingerprint.

Authoring: *TAP > Planet visuals*:
- Bake all: surface, weather and sky.
- Bake weather only.
- Bake atmosphere only.
- Rebuild material library.
- Bake satellite detail.

Texture arrays and 3D cloud noise are generated by importers (`TextureArrayImporter` for `.texarray`, `CloudNoiseImporter` for `.cloudnoise`) from versioned source images or seeds. Sky lookups are EXR. New profile controls: Sky Brightness, Weather Mode, Cloud Optical Depth, Cloud Billow
Scale, Satellite Albedo, Satellite Detail, Ocean Shallow Colour.

Limits: the launch plain near the pad is flat by design and has no vegetation scatter; near-ground variety beyond the
photographed materials and satellite octaves is future work. Earth's cloud systems are scaled onto a planet 10.6 times
smaller (a hurricane spans tens of kilometres). The Windows player was not rebuilt in this pass, and no controlled
frame-rate benchmark was run.

Approved 2026-09-30. Implemented for Tellus and Luma, including detailed map bodies. Visual review and hardware performance acceptance remain separate from implementation.

## Delivered

- Body-specific visual profiles and a shared material/noise library, with generated fallback rendering for bodies without assets.
- 4096x2048 surface colour, normal and material/ocean maps for Tellus and Luma. Geography comes from the existing deterministic terrain.
- Six CC0 2048 ground material sets in mipmapped BC7 texture arrays: grass, forest ground, sand, rock, snow and lunar regolith. Body-fixed triplanar sampling adds near-ground detail without changing terrain heights or colliders.
- Ocean wave normals, roughness, glint and shoreline blending; deterministic instanced nearby visual stones. Stones have no collision or gameplay effect.
- Spherical texture clouds and ray-marched near volumes, sharing a 4096x2048 weather map and 64-cubed shape/erosion texture sampled in decorrelated domains. Cloud-top erosion gives billows different heights. Direct-light extinction and projected surface shadows are included.
- Rayleigh/Mie/ozone scattering, a 128x64 sunlight transmittance LUT, aerial perspective and a blue atmospheric limb. Sunlight tint/extinction also reaches surface and spacecraft lighting.
- Map bodies sample real terrain at 256x128 subdivisions, clamp oceans to datum and share detailed surface maps. Map clouds use layers; biome diagnostics hide clouds and atmosphere and retain biome colours.
- **Esc > Planet graphics:** Low/Medium/High/Ultra, independent Off/Layers/Volumetric selector, and surface rocks toggle. Changes apply immediately and persist independently of game saves. The factory default is High/Volumetric/rocks; the user's existing Low/Layers/rocks preference was restored after these diagnostics.
- Authoring: **TAP > Planet visuals > Bake all**, **Bake weather only**, and **Install renderer**. Bake keys validate terrain, dimensions, weather seed/coverage, profile version and scattering inputs; stale assets fall back rather than painting old geography onto edited terrain. Weather-only baking preserves surface assets and body definitions.

Ultra uses 80 cloud integration samples; High uses 48, at half screen resolution with depth-aware reconstruction. All presets share current 4K orbital/2K material assets; Low biases ground mips. Optional 8K global maps and selected 4K ground assets remain an authoring upgrade after hardware profiling; they are not currently loaded or advertised as delivered.

## Integration

Unity 6000.5.7f1 / URP 17.5.0 is retained, with no added packages or paid dependency. The PC renderer keeps SSAO; both renderer assets include the new Render Graph feature, installed through Editor APIs.

Each cloud body belongs to one view: local flight, scaled far camera, or map. Local and scaled depth remain separate through the overlay depth clear. Geometry depth is converted to physical ray distance. Volumes smoothly become filtered texture clouds across altitudes 0.035-0.2 body radii (21-120 km on Tellus), well before the local/scaled camera handover; map uses texture clouds directly. Atmospheric bodies outside the frustum are rejected before requesting intermediate colour/depth. Texture-only views allocate no temporal cloud textures and perform no cloud history copies.

The atmosphere/cloud composite runs before transparent geometry and final post-processing. Plume emission then receives only transmission between the eye and its actual fragment. Background clouds cannot dim a nearby exhaust plume. The composite preserves camera MSAA to match the depth attachment reused by URP's transparent pass.

Integration uses explicit graph textures and per-camera/body imported RTHandle history. History resets on camera discontinuities, origin shifts, large UT jumps, resolution/graphics changes, map transitions and scene teardown. No graph TextureHandle is retained between frames. Runtime resources have explicit owners and cleanup.

Terrain generation, biome identities, launch-site flattening, collision geometry, orbital mechanics, physics atmosphere and game-save format are unchanged. Existing ProjectSettings modifications were preserved. Unity regenerated its dynamic TextMeshPro font atlas during rendering/build checks.

## Cloud-cover and repetition correction (2026-09-30)

Tellus cloud coverage is now 0.38 (previously 0.46). A narrower weather transition leaves larger clear regions in flight and map views. Volumetric density is explicitly multiplied by weather coverage, preventing isolated clouds from appearing in clear weather. ## Regional cloud layout refinement (2026-10-01)

The reference feedback identified an even field of small clouds. Fine noise had dominated the broad weather field. The weather baker now lays out seven broad cloud banks, eight smaller broken patches and six thin fronts in body-fixed spherical coordinates. Curved regional envelopes, domain distortion, gaps and kilometre-scale puff detail provide different sizes without distributing speckles over the whole planet. Smaller patches vary in optical strength; the thin fronts use lower strength. These are deterministic art features, not a meteorological simulation.

The 4K weather texture stores continuous optical coverage, including faint edges and wisps, before building trilinear mipmaps. Weather bake keys now include algorithm version 4. Near-volume noise uses a common shape threshold and scales extinction by optical coverage; thin weather no longer vanishes because its low opacity raises the shape threshold. The transparent transmission shader shares this helper. The single-sample distant/map fallback and distance blend are retained. Regional layout adds no runtime noise lookups, ray steps or draw calls.

The weather-only bake preserved surface, terrain and atmospheric LUT assets. Three matched globe before/after pairs are linked in the [review gallery](D:/Dev/KSP/Screenshots/PlanetFeedback/GALLERY.md).
